# Review cards

Leitner boxes. Box 1 comes back next session, box 2 after 2 sessions, box 3 after 4, box 4 after 8. Right: up one box. Wrong: back to box 1. Confident (3) and wrong: box 1 plus a ⚑ flag.

The learner writes cards at each exit ticket, in their own words. Claude adds a card for every checkpoint miss and every time it had to give an answer outright. Claude picks the 3 due cards for do-now, mixing topics, and updates Box and Due after each answer.
